Driver: Can I use a pallet jack?
Elias: No, the cobblestone and the width of the alley rule that out.
Driver: A wheelbarrow?
Elias: That is not the prescribed method for this delivery.
Site foreman: The note requires hand carry.
Elias: Yes, in small loads.
Driver: So I split up the brick stacks.
Elias: Exactly, to protect the cobblestone.
Site foreman: The lime bags have to stay under the covered stoop.
Driver: I will also keep the historic gate clear.
Elias: Yes.
Site foreman: The liftgate is allowed at another site, not here.
Driver: And the Navy Yard forklift is beside the point.
Elias: Correct.
Site foreman: Name Society Hill in the plan.
Driver: I want a short instruction I can use on site.
Elias: That is the goal.
Site foreman: Do not mix the modern access options with this historic alley.
